Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: polished mobile application concept board.
Primary request: Create exactly ONE finished, high fidelity raster concept board showing TWO portrait Android app screens side by side for NearKey, the product where your phone acts as a nearby key to sign in on paired websites. Concept 05 is "Forest dark".
Scene/backdrop: wide landscape board on a neutral charcoal surrounding canvas, quiet and understated. Tiny editorial label above the pair reading "05 / FOREST DARK". Each screen is straight-on, flat, beautifully crisp and fully legible, no perspective. The panels occupy most of the board, generous even margins, aligned top and bottom. Thin understated rounded phone edge and native Android status bar with 9:41, signal, wifi, battery; bottom gesture bar. No hands, no desk, no extraneous decoration.
Style: production-quality mobile UI design, restrained premium utility app. Deep forest-black #10261f page surfaces, lifted dark-green #1b342a cards, off-white #f1f6ed type, subtle thin green #345443 borders. Soft mint #d9f1c7 primary buttons with dark forest #174b3b labels. Forest brand #174b3b and ink #153c30 translated into dark mode. No neon, no cyber aesthetic, no glow. Manrope-like semibold headings, DM Sans-like body with clean smooth letters, readable hierarchy. Consistent 24px inner margins, 16px corner radii, ample spacing and at least 48px tall tap targets.
Brand: nearKey wordmark (exact capitalization: n e a r K e y), next to a minimal lowercase n mark in a rounded square. Use nearKey once at top of each screen.
